# Cavinova

Build a sophisticated, responsive, multipage website for Cavinova — Advanced Acoustic Aesthetics, a proposed skincare technology venture developed as part of the Rutgers University Master of Business and Science (MBS) Capstone program.

Cavinova is exploring the commercialization of emerging intellectual property involving ultrasound-assisted topical delivery using acoustically responsive microbubble formulations.
The website should communicate Cavinova's proposed technology, product concept, commercial opportunity, founding team, and research-driven approach.

Critical context: Cavinova is currently a hypothetical, pre-commercial venture developed for an academic commercialization project. It is not an established operating company. The proposed products have not been commercially launched, and the team has not established ownership or licensing rights to the underlying patent. The goal is to create a credible representation of the proposed company and business opportunity while remaining transparent about its academic and developmental status.

- Primary Brand Logo: Use the Cavinova logo asset with acoustic frequency wave motif and tagline "ADVANCED ACOUSTIC AESTHETICS".
- Brand Tagline / Motto: "SCIENCE IN HARMONY WITH SKIN"

- Typography: Elegant serif headers (Playfair Display / Cormorant Garamond style) paired with clean, modern sans-serif body copy (Inter / Plus Jakarta Sans).
- Clearly structured image and asset placeholder cards with dashed accent borders, icon badges, and helpful descriptions so the user can easily drop in photos, 3D renders, and diagrams later.

Sitemap and Navigation (6 fully functional pages):
1. Home — /
- Hero section featuring the official Cavinova identity, positioning statement, "Conceptual Venture • Rutgers MBS Capstone" tag, brand motto "Science in Harmony with Skin", and CTA buttons to explore the science and opportunity.
- The Core Challenge: Stratum corneum barrier limitations in conventional topical skincare.
- The Proposed Solution: Ultrasound-assisted delivery paired with acoustically responsive microbubbles (sonophoresis & acoustic cavitation).
- Three-pillar synergy: Advanced Ultrasound Applicator + Bio-Acoustic Serum Formulations + Professional In-Clinic Protocol.
- High-level commercial snapshot ($45,000 console target, $35 per-treatment consumable kit, high recurring gross margin).
- Clear pathways to /science, /platform, and /opportunity.

2. Science & Technology — /science
- Explain the scientific foundation: Stratum corneum barrier physics, low-frequency/focused ultrasound, sonophoresis, and acoustic cavitation.
- Interactive Step-by-Step Mechanism Visualizer:
  * Step 1: Topical Application of microbubble-infused active serum (hyaluronic acid, antioxidants, peptides).
  * Step 2: Acoustic Activation via controlled ultrasound handpiece.
  * Step 3: Microbubble Oscillations & Cavitation creating transient, reversible micro-pathways in the lipid bilayer.
  * Step 4: Enhanced Active Permeation deep into viable epidermal layers without tissue damage.
- Rigorous Scientific Transparency: Clear callout distinguishing established sonophoresis physics from the specific lip/skin delivery mechanisms claimed in U.S. Patent App. Pub. No. US20250073439A1 (John H. Shadduck), noting clinical validation is ongoing and under evaluation.

3. Product Concept — /platform
- Conceptual Product Architecture: Interactive hardware & consumable breakdown:
  * Ergonomic Ultrasound Applicator / Handpiece (piezoelectric transducer assembly, optimized resonant frequency).
  * Smart Touchscreen Control Console (pre-programmed clinical protocols, energy regulation).
  * Single-Use Acoustic Treatment Tips with NFC/RFID authentication (sterility, cross-contamination prevention, recurring consumable).
  * Cavinova Active Serum Cartridges (acoustically responsive lipid-shelled microbubbles + dermatological actives).
- Treatment Workflow (Preparation -> Calibration -> Acoustic Infusion -> Post-Treatment Nourishment).
- Labeled prominently: "Conceptual Product Visualization — Not a Commercial Product" (no claim of physical working prototype or FDA clearance).
- Design and engineering placeholder cards highlighting planned testing and biocompatibility verification.

4. Commercial Opportunity — /opportunity
- Target Market: 4,184+ eligible U.S. Medical Spas & Dermatology / High-End Aesthetic practices.
- Market Analysis: TAM, SAM, and SOM metrics grounded in the business plan data.
- Razor-and-Blade Business Model: Capital equipment sales/leases ($45,000 unit target) paired with high-margin recurring consumable treatment kits ($35 wholesale cost to provider, supporting a $250 end-user patient treatment).
- Provider Unit Economics Calculator / Interactive Table: Showing monthly treatment volume (5, 10, 25, 50 treatments/month), revenue generation ($1,250 to $12,500/mo), consumable costs, equipment lease payback, and net clinic margin.
- 5-Year Staged Commercialization Roadmap (Years 1 to 5 stage gates from IP Option, Feasibility, Prototyping & Regulatory Testing, Pilot Manufacturing, to Commercial Scale).

5. Founding Team — /team

  * Victoria DeMeola — Co-Founder, Technology & R&D Lead (Biotechnology, genomics, molecular diagnostics, lab operations, foundational technology evaluation).
